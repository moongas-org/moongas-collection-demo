Bright as the stars,
Rise and fall
Do you ever wonder
Where it all starts?
Believe it;
Wear the cloak.
Swimming above,
Watching the flood
Bright as the stars
Rise and fall
Do you ever wonder
Where do we start?
Believe it;
The rain will come
Another night,
Another day.
Everywhere we look
Lights are moving faster,
And then,
You see it there,
Slowly holding on,
Colours fill your mind,
And voices,
Singing our song,
And voices,
Singing along
Here we are,
Standing tall,
Waiting and hoping.
See the lights shut (or last shard?)
Over me,
Over the water.
Another day goes by,
Love in your eyes
Stare patiently
To see the sky.
Then what?
(People walking by
You smile as you walk by)
