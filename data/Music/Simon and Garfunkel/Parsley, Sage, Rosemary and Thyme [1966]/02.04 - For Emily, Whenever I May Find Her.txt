What a dream I had, pressed in work and deed, Clothed in criminal hand of smoky burgundy,
Softed in the rain, I wandered empty streets down past the shop displays,
I heard cathedral bells dripping down the alleyways as I walked on,
And when you ran to me, your cheeks flushed with the night,
We walked on frosted fields of juniper and lamp-light, I held your hand.
And when I walked and felt you warm in here, I kissed your honey-head with my grateful tears,
Oh, I love you girl, oh, I love you.
