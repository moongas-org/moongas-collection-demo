We had intended just calling the album Tales of Mystery and Imagination,
but the record company specifically asked us to have an artistic identification,
so we called it the Alan Parsons Project,
and people in the industry and the public appeared to think of this as being a band.
This was quite fortuitous because during the making of the album,
we realised that there was more scope for this kind of musical venture,
and we developed many other ideas for making albums based on different themes.
As Edgar Allan Poe had been described as the father of science fiction,
it seemed reasonably logical that we should perhaps go into the science fiction area
for the next album, and the result was the iRobot album.
