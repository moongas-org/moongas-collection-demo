Now so many are taking vacations to the mountains, the lakes, or the sea, where they rest from
their cares and their worries. What a wonderful time that must be, but it seems not my luck
to be like them. I must toil through the heat and the cold, seeking out the lost sheep-wong,
the mountains, bringing wonderers back to the fold.
When I take my vacation in heaven, what a wonderful time it will be.
Hearing concerts by the heavenly chorals, and the face of my Savior I'll see, sitting down by the banks of the river,
nath the shade of the evergreen tree. I shall rest from my burdens forever. Won't you spend your vacation with me?
