As black night fast approaches dawn, we gather in the mist by the naman.
As the stones are struck by the sun's first rays, we feel the earth power in our veins,
the awesome power that fills the stones, we feel it in our minds and bones, and as
we dance around the ring, joy fills our souls, we start to sing, all power
awakens and growing fast, an awesome force from deep in the past, yet there are
those who do not understand and the stones are desecrated by their hands.
On such group of stones facing desecration at the hands of these people
is the standing stone complex of Kalanesh on the Isle of Lewis in Scotland,
sometimes referred to as the Stonehenge of the North. This unique arrangement of stones
is facing the same kind of rape that happened at Stonehenge. There are now plans afoot to construct
a hideous, unsightly visitor centre there, thus ruining the whole aura of the place,
along with plans to build a bigger road and even to charge an admission fee.
Who are these people? When their arrogance think that they can deny the people access to our
own temples. Stone circles belong to all of us, not simply to the powers that be.
Remember, these are the same people who seriously considered carpeting Stonehenge with
Astroturf and even considered constructing a plastic replica foamhenge beside the real monument.
Such people are not fit guardians of these places. Free the stones. Free the stones.
