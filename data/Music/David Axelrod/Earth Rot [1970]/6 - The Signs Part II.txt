And the passes are wintering away.
The clouds, when the fields will be free.
The trees, when the rainforests are new.
And soon there will be no trees.
Life is buried from the sea.
The waves are all dead or dry.
No marks, fresh rivers are found.
And the plants and trees are dying, dying.
One rich empire need even best.
As the water becomes more divine.
There will be nothing that's clean to drink.
For those who pray, the laws of nature.
