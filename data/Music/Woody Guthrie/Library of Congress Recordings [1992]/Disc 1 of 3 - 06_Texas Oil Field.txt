And times had got pretty hard in Oklahoma and everybody was drifting out that way,
so I drifted out there with them.
And when I got out into that country, I got a job about the third day I was there.
I got a job with a fella that owned a root beer stand, supposedly,
and he said he'd give me $3 a day to stand behind the counter and sell people root beers.
So I told him I had intelligence enough to do that, so I got around behind the counter.
And he told me, he said now in addition to this root beer,
he said, here's some bottles here of another description.
He says if anybody comes up and lays a dollar and a half on the counter here,
why you reach down and gently and firmly let them have one of these, your bottles.

just purity old and unadulterated corn whiskey.

and a lot of times there wouldn't be any customers in the place.
